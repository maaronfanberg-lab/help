Fermi–Pasta–Ulam–Tsingou β latticeRK4 · physical impulses

Neighboring masses interact through nonlinear springs: force = kΔ + β₄Δ³. An optional double-well potential creates switching. Mass defects scatter pulses; the measured energy includes kinetic, bond, and on-site terms. Tap opposite ends to create collisions. This is a general FPUT-β model, not the friction equation of the excitable paper.

Charged particles in a magnetic trap2D · classical Lorentz / Coulomb

Classical point charges obey electric, softened Coulomb and Lorentz forces with optional confinement and contact impulses. Boris-style magnetic rotation reduces unphysical magnetic-field energy injection. Colored particles mark charge sign, not imagined quantum behavior. This is NOT a CERN collision simulator.
