About this experimental equation

The numerical engine uses a finite nine-state approximation. Input gematria values are assigned to α, γ, κ, ζ, ε and the initial states; nonlinear coupling, delayed interactions and memory evolve the states. The final magnitudes are scaled and rounded before reverse gematria. This does not solve the original infinite-dimensional fractional, spectral, geometric, and stochastic equation exactly. Matching uses a limited embedded Hebrew vocabulary; results are marked exact or nearest.
